What the app adds

Offline is the big one. The rest is a nicer fit for a phone in the car.

  • Works without signal. Every big thing ships inside the app, and photos you have seen are kept for next time.
  • What's near me. One tap frames your location and your five closest big things.
  • Save a shortlist. Heart the ones you want and show only those. The list stays on your device.
  • Plan the road trip. Add stops, put them in order, see the drive time between them, and hand the route to Apple Maps.
  • Made for iPhone Duo. On the dual-screen iPhone Duo it uses both displays: the outer screen when closed, and the list beside the details when open. iPad gets the same side-by-side layout.
  • Honest pins. Same as here: a dashed outline means the pin marks the town, not the sculpture.

Same data

The app uses the dataset behind this site, built from open sources and corrected where they were wrong. It checks for updates when you open it and never replaces what it has with a bad download. How the dataset was put together, and how precise each pin is, is on the About page.
